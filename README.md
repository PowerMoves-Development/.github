## 👋 Welcome to PowerMoves Development

**PowerMoves Development** is the development division of **PowerMoves Gaming**, dedicated to creating, improving, and maintaining custom resources for immersive multiplayer roleplay experiences.

Our work combines practical gameplay experience with creative problem-solving to develop resources that enhance functionality, improve interaction, and make virtual worlds feel more alive.

We believe the best development ideas often come directly from the people who play the game.

## 💻 What We Do

Our development interests include:

- **Custom Scripts & Resources** — Original gameplay features, interactions, and utilities.
- **FiveM Development** — Server-side and client-side resources designed for roleplay environments.
- **QBCore Integration** — Development focused primarily on the QBCore ecosystem.
- **Lua Scripting** — Custom logic, functionality, and resource enhancements.
- **User Experience** — Practical interfaces and intuitive player interactions.
- **Open-Source Collaboration** — Learning from, contributing to, and respecting the broader development community.

## 🎮 Part of PowerMoves Gaming

PowerMoves Development operates as part of the broader **PowerMoves Gaming** community.

Our projects are shaped by firsthand roleplay experience, community feedback, and a commitment to creating engaging multiplayer environments.

While FiveM remains our primary development focus, we continue to explore opportunities across other gaming platforms.

## 🛠️ Development Philosophy

> **Build with purpose. Design for players. Improve through experience.**

We believe great resources should be:

- **Functional** — Solving real gameplay needs.
- **Intuitive** — Easy for players to understand and use.
- **Reliable** — Developed with stability and performance in mind.
- **Thoughtful** — Designed around believable and enjoyable roleplay.
- **Maintainable** — Organized for future improvements and continued development.

Our goal isn't simply to add more features. It's to create meaningful experiences.

## 📂 Our Repositories

This organization serves as the home for PowerMoves Development projects, including original scripts, utilities, and development resources.

Some repositories may contain experimental work, historical development references, or projects that are not intended for general release.

**Please review each repository's documentation, license, and development status before using or distributing its contents.**

## 🤝 Community & Collaboration

We appreciate the developers, creators, and contributors who help make the FiveM and open-source communities what they are.

We welcome constructive discussion, shared ideas, and opportunities to collaborate on projects that improve roleplay experiences.
